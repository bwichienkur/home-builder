## Bottom line from the picture

1. **Weighted pipeline $22.65M** = confidence × min style total; funnel Lead ~$46.3M is the unweighted opportunity min stack.  
2. **WIP $18.74M** = sum of PM WIP = Open WIP.  
3. **% complete is billing-based** (footer says so).  
4. **Daily logs in the art are monthly fractions**, not the later 4-week standard.  
5. **Monique is included as a PM** in the concept scorecard.  
6. **Past due appears twice (24 vs 36)** — don’t trust either as gospel without reconciling the mock.  
7. Funnel stages + Signed Backlog (WIP+Closed) + 90-day bars are first-class in the concept even if BT Open leads alone can’t fill Proposal→Contract today.
